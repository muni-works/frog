verdict · pmorg-first default, toggle as "grouping" setting

The org tree is frog's identity and the needs-you lens works better with it. But the time reading is genuinely calmer and cam should judge with eyes on real content — hence this mock. Toggle lives in the sidebar's view-options menu (the existing filter button), three values: org · time · off.

notethe time variant changes nesting

In time-buckets, nested workers don't fold lines — they appear as flat rows in Running, or drop out (their parent speaks for them). That's a real behavioral difference, not just ordering: org grouping = where things live; time grouping = what's alive.

noteboth share the dot language

Same 7pt dots, same needs-you badge, same account pill. The toggle changes sections and ordering only, so neither read loses state. Sessions always keep their tree position in the transcript's worker lines.
