**what i'd do (read-only):**
- `git diff --stat`, then `git diff server/docker-compose.yml`, to see exactly which pins changed.
- compare that with the PR's compose hunks. do they touch the same lines, or just different services?

- if the hunks overlap, the pull fails or leaves a conflict on a live host.
