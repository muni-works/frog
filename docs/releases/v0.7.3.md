frog 0.7.3. Skills, Connections, Rules and Memory move into Settings, local models show in
the web's model menu, and sending from far up a conversation always lands on the reply, on
top of v0.7.2 (Nov 19). 0.7.2 was tagged but never published, so if you are coming from
0.7.1, its notes apply too: [v0.7.2](v0.7.2.md).

## Install

```sh
curl -fsSL https://frog.toys/install.sh | sh   # CLI, macOS arm64 + Linux x86_64/aarch64
```

The Mac app: [frog.toys/download](https://frog.toys/download). It updates itself (Sparkle).

## Settings

- **Customize is gone as a separate mode.** Skills, Connections, Rules, Memory and
  Automations are sections of Settings, between the app's own sections and your account.
  The sidebar is New session, Search and Settings. Old links to Customize still work.
  (#491)

## Composer

- **Local models in the web's model menu.** Under "Run it on your Mac" the menu lists the
  local models at $0 a token, from the same list the Mac uses. Picking one opens Frog for
  Mac at Settings › Models, or the download page. It does not carry your message across
  yet. (#493)

## Transcript

- **Sending from far up always lands on the reply.** On the Mac the scroll could stop short
  of the end, about three sends in seven in our runs. (#492)
- **"Remember that …" only at the start of a message.** A pasted document with that phrase
  inside no longer saves a note. (#495)
- **A suggestion you accept is not something you typed.** Pressing Tab on Frog's suggested
  next message and sending it no longer counts as your own words, so it cannot save a note
  by itself. Edit it and it is yours. (#495)
- `/memory` and `/recall` answer again in a child session on the Mac. `/remember` and
  `/forget` work where you address Frog directly: the app, the terminal, an editor, and
  `frog -p`. (#494, #495)

## Built, not switched on

- **Frog noting things on its own** and **changing settings when you ask in a conversation**
  are drawn in both apps and off in both. Four reviews tightened how a change is approved:
  anything that writes text is a card you approve, only text you typed counts as you asking,
  child sessions get neither, and a receipt means the change took effect. They stay off
  until the same holds for cloud sessions and the phone. (#487, #489, #490, #494, #496)

## Known

- On the web, Settings › Memory shows a "Notes on its own" switch although Frog does not
  note things on its own yet.
- On the web, recalling a sent message with the up arrow and sending it again counts as
  typed even when it began as an accepted suggestion.
- Automations is an empty page: there is no scheduler yet.
- The explainer cards' "How … works" links point at documentation that isn't written yet.
- The iOS app does not build from this version.
