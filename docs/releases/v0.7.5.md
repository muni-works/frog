frog 0.7.5. An energy cap for this Mac, local models from the web open on the Mac, and replies
that stream in place, on top of v0.7.4 (Nov 20).

## Install

```sh
curl -fsSL https://frog.toys/install.sh | sh   # CLI, macOS arm64 + Linux x86_64/aarch64
```

The Mac app: [frog.toys/download](https://frog.toys/download). It updates itself (Sparkle).

## Energy on this Mac

For off-grid and garage setups. (#506)

- **Daily energy cap.** Settings › Usage › Energy: None, or 10 to 500 Wh. It counts this Mac's
  measured energy since midnight. At the cap, models on this Mac pause with "Raise Cap…", the
  same way cloud models pause at the monthly cap. A turn under way finishes.
- **Power limit.** None, or 10 to 100 W, or `power_limit_w` in `config.toml`. Before each local
  model call, frog waits off energy drawn above the limit ("Holding to 40 W", at most 2 minutes).
  It holds the average over a few calls, not peak draw. Cloud calls are never held, and tok/s is
  timed after the wait.

## From the web to the Mac

- **Pick a local model on frog.toys** and Frog for Mac opens a new session with that model
  chosen and your message waiting in the composer. It's never sent for you. If the model isn't
  on this Mac yet, the session uses your usual one and says why; a link never starts a
  download. The link is `frog://new?model=…&prompt=…&repo=…`. (#505)
- Without the Mac app, the web sends you to the download page.

## The transcript

- **A reply streams where it rests.** No card while it writes, so nothing jumps when it
  finishes, on the Mac and the web. (#507)
- **The web writes replies in word by word,** as the Mac does, with the same green pen, and
  half-finished markdown waits until it's whole. (#510)
- **Tool icons and list markers line up** with the left edge of the text. (#507)
- **A stopped turn keeps its footer.** It says Stopped, then the time, tokens and cost, and
  counts what the stop cut off. (#508)
