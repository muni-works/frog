frog 0.7.1. One transcript and one Settings on the Mac and the web, a calmer start for cloud
sessions, and the credit wall, on top of v0.7.0 (Nov 18).

## Install

```sh
curl -fsSL https://frog.toys/install.sh | sh   # CLI, macOS arm64 + Linux x86_64/aarch64
```

The Mac app: [frog.toys/download](https://frog.toys/download). It updates itself (Sparkle).

## One transcript

The Mac and the web now draw a conversation from one design: the same type, spacing, rows
and words, measured against each other to within a point.

- **A live thinking row.** While the model thinks, one line shows the gist of the current
  thought and changes in place with a cross-fade. Under it, a stats line: time, tokens and
  tokens per second. (#426, #428, #430)
- **Quieter work.** Steps are lines with one left edge, long runs fold, and a test run reads
  "10 of 11" with the failing names. A failed step says Failed in the verb. (#420, #421,
  #424)
- **The turn line.** At the right of the stats line, a small line shows the turn's pace.
  Click it for the turn's timeline: where the time went between thinking, writing and
  tools. (#427, #429, #431)
- **"Thought for 12s" is right for older conversations,** which used to count the writing
  time as thinking. (#441, #444, #446)

## One Settings

- **Settings on the Mac is a mode of the window,** like Documentation: ⌘, turns the sidebar
  into the sections, esc goes back. The separate Settings window is gone. (#439, #442, #445)
- **The same nine sections on both:** General, Appearance, Models, Permissions, Connections,
  Shortcuts, Usage, Account, Billing, drawn from one list of rows. (#432, #436)
- **On the web, a setting that needs your Mac shows as an invitation** to get the Mac app,
  under "On your Mac". (#436)
- **Models:** "New conversations use" is one choice, the last model you used or a named
  one. "New messages use" is removed; a conversation keeps its model. (#442)

## Frog Cloud

- **A new session starts on one steady view.** The sent message stays put and one row says
  how far the sandbox has got, then carries on as the turn's live row. Before, the web
  passed through six states, one of them "Session not found". (#447, #452)
- **The credit wall.** A quiet warning when credit is nearly spent. Out of credit, the
  conversation says the work is saved and what the session cost, and adding credit or a
  code resumes the turn where it stopped. (#438, #440)
- **Preview on the web.** A cloud session's Preview tab shows what the sandbox serves.
  (#422)

## Fixes

- Dragging a control inside a visual no longer drags the window. (#433)
- The Mac transcript no longer hangs when a new turn starts while the view is updating.
  (#435)

## For measuring, off by default

- The apps can report onboarding milestones (first session, first result, connecting
  GitHub). Nothing is sent unless a key is configured, and the Mac asks first. No prompts,
  code, file names or email addresses are ever sent. (#437)
