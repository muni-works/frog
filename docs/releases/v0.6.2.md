frog 0.6.2. Documentation inside the app, on top of v0.6.1 (Nov 16).

## Install

```sh
curl -fsSL https://frog.toys/install.sh | sh   # CLI, macOS arm64 + Linux x86_64/aarch64
```

The Mac app: [frog.toys/download](https://frog.toys/download). It updates itself (Sparkle).

## Documentation, in the app

- **A Documentation row in the sidebar.** On the Mac and on the web, the bottom of the left
  sidebar opens the docs in the same window: the thread list becomes the page list, the main
  area shows the page, and "Back to app" (or Escape) returns you to the thread, scroll
  position and draft you left. A running turn keeps running. On the Mac it is also under
  Help. (#385, #386)
- **Sixteen pages to start.** Install, your first session, picking a model; permissions and
  auto mode, web search and fetch, sessions and children, memory and `FROG.md`, skills and
  MCP; models on your Mac, your own nodes, your own keys, Frog Cloud; three pages for when
  something's off (no web results, a refused fetch, a thread that just stopped); and the
  changelog. Each page shows what applies to the app you're in. (#386)
- **Checked against the code.** Every page was fact-checked against the engine and the
  apps before it shipped, and 19 statements were corrected. The docs describe this version.
- **Search filters page titles.** Full-text search, the generated reference pages and a
  public docs.frog.toys come later. (#381)

## Smaller things

- **Scrolling up a long thread is lighter on the Mac.** Loading older messages used to
  build a screenful of rows nobody could see; each load now keeps the top row in place.
  Peak rows on screen went from 35 to 38 down to 20, and the worst frame from 72 to 84 ms
  down to 55 to 59 ms, measured on a loaded Mac. (#393)
- **The transcript drops rows that scroll away.** A row that left the screen stayed live in
  the background until its slot was reused; it is now released at once. (#389)
- **A stalled load of older messages leaves a trace** in the log, for the next time one
  doesn't arrive. (#391)

## Tests

- Mac test runs no longer put windows on screen or take the keyboard, and the test host uses
  a home folder and settings of its own, not yours. (#389, #392)
- The Mac suite's CI step keeps its results and names each failing assertion. (#384)

The engine is unchanged from 0.6.1.
