frog 0.7.4. Frog on a phone, plans you can change in one tap, and a meter that says what you
have left, on top of v0.7.3 (Nov 19).

## Install

```sh
curl -fsSL https://frog.toys/install.sh | sh   # CLI, macOS arm64 + Linux x86_64/aarch64
```

The Mac app: [frog.toys/download](https://frog.toys/download). It updates itself (Sparkle).

## On a phone

The web app at frog.toys has a phone layout.

- **The conversation is the screen.** The title is a button; tap it for a sheet with your
  sessions, search, a new session and Settings. Desktop window controls are gone on touch.
  (#499)
- **Settings is a list you drill into,** and Billing fits the screen. (#499, #501)
- **Add to Home Screen** gives a standalone app. (#499)

## What you have left, and how to get more

- **The meter says it in words.** In the composer: "21¢ left" on free credit, "12% of week"
  on a plan. Tap it for your plan, or go to frog.toys/pay. On the web and on a phone. (#499)
- **Change plans in place.** Billing and the plan sheet show all three plans with your own
  marked. "Upgrade to Max" changes the plan you have; Stripe prorates it. Cancel is a link
  underneath. (#501)
- **A plan is its price and its weekly work.** Plans no longer list how many sessions run at
  once; every account has the same generous ceiling. (#501)
- **After your first task on free credit,** one card says what it cost and what a plan
  covers. Once. (#499)
- **Out of credit,** the wall offers a plan first, then credit. (#499)

## Fixes

- The repo menu's "Scratch sandbox" no longer loses its first letters on a narrow phone.
  (#500)
- On the web, recalling a sent message with the up arrow keeps whether you typed it or
  accepted it as a suggestion. (#498)
- Settings › Memory on the web no longer shows a "Notes on its own" switch for a feature
  that is not on yet. (#498)
- In a child session, "remember that …" says why nothing was saved. Tapping the Mac
  notification for a proposed change opens that session. (#498)
- The iOS app builds again. (#498)
- Frog Cloud: the repo list no longer fails for a minute after a cancelled request; a plan
  bought seconds ago is manageable at once; a revoked key stays revoked; a top-up is
  credited exactly once.

## Built, not switched on

- **Frog noting things on its own** and **changing settings when you ask in a conversation**
  are still off in every app. They hold for local sessions on the Mac; cloud sessions and
  two devices sharing one session are not done.

## Known

- Picking a local model in the web's model menu opens Frog for Mac but does not carry your
  message across.
- Automations is an empty page: there is no scheduler yet.
- The explainer cards' "How … works" links point at documentation that isn't written yet.
