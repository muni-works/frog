frog 0.6.4. Flush sidebars and a cleaner first run, on top of v0.6.3 (Nov 17).

## Install

```sh
curl -fsSL https://frog.toys/install.sh | sh   # CLI, macOS arm64 + Linux x86_64/aarch64
```

The Mac app: [frog.toys/download](https://frog.toys/download). It updates itself (Sparkle).

## Sidebars

- **Flush to the window.** On the Mac and the web, the left sidebar and the right inspector
  are full-height columns that touch the window's edges, with a hairline against the page.
  The floating rounded cards and the margin around them are gone, so the conversation gets
  the space back. (#401, #402)
- **The Mac's sidebar header is in the title row,** beside the traffic lights: the frog, the
  view options and the collapse button. The list starts with New session. (#402)
- **Collapsed means gone on the Mac.** The narrow rail is removed. The sidebar toggle and New
  session wait beside the traffic lights; the toggle carries a dot while a conversation waits
  on you, and resting the pointer on it lists the live ones. Moving the pointer to the
  window's left edge peeks the list over the page. (#402)
- **The Mac's inspector is one column, like the web's.** Changes, Terminal and Preview fill
  the height of the window. Closed means gone, reopened from the toolbar or with ⌥⌘0; the
  "+N −M" pill is removed, since the composer's status line shows the same counts. (#402)

## Models

- **This Mac's models are back in the Mac's model menu.** It opens on This Mac (Apple
  Intelligence, what you've downloaded, and one model to get that suits this Mac, each "$0 a
  token"), then your machines, then Frog Cloud with each model's price. (#400, #403)
- **Signed out, Frog Cloud's rows say "Sign in"** and picking one signs you in and moves the
  chat to that model. (#403)
- **The web's model menu** shows input and output prices to three figures, puts the default
  first, and leaves out the small helper models. (#400)

## First run

Found by walking a first run on the CLI, the Mac and the web
(`docs/design/first-run/README.md`). (#400)

- **Running out of credit on the web works again.** The "out of credit" card had stopped
  matching the engine's message, so the turn just said it stopped and Continue was refused.
- **The Mac's permission card says where the step runs.** It said "this Mac" in Frog Cloud
  sessions too.
- **The CLI points a new user at `frog login`,** says when it's waiting for the browser,
  lists its commands under `frog --help`, and the installer ends with the next step.
- **The pricing page's DeepSeek row** showed another model's numbers; it shows its own.
- Retry and stand-in notices show on the Mac, running rows say "Working…", and the web's
  repo menu says why it's empty when it is.
