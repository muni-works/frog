frog 0.7.2. One window chrome on the Mac and the web, a new sidebar and home, Skills,
Connections, Rules and Memory you can see, and a transcript that lands where you're looking,
on top of v0.7.1 (Nov 19).

## Install

```sh
curl -fsSL https://frog.toys/install.sh | sh   # CLI, macOS arm64 + Linux x86_64/aarch64
```

The Mac app: [frog.toys/download](https://frog.toys/download). It updates itself (Sparkle).

## One window

- **The same chrome on both apps.** One capsule of window controls at the top right, a turn
  rail beside the conversation, and one header: the title is the session's menu, and you
  rename a session in place. (#462, #463, #464)
- **The header is a surface.** Settings and the other mode pages no longer scroll through the
  title row, and a page's own button sits clear of the window controls. (#476)

## Sidebar and home

- **A new sidebar on both apps.** New session, Search and Settings at the top, each with an
  icon. With no sessions yet, the list offers a few things to start with. At the foot: one
  quiet pill for what needs saying (the Mac app, low credit, an update), your account, and
  the Pricing, Privacy and Terms links. (#468)
- **Pinned sessions on the Mac** can be named, each row has one slot for what matters now,
  and hovering a session shows a card with where it stands. (#459)
- **The web's home is one question.** "What should we work on?", a composer, and one
  suggestion at a time that Tab accepts. Signed out, a single card says what Frog is. (#461)
- **Choose a folder works on the Mac.** It had been wired to "no folder". (#478)
- **A short repo list.** The where menu shows your best six repos, the one you used last
  first, and searches the rest. A repo spelled in another case is one row. (#478, #485)

## What Frog knows and can do

A new Customize mode on both apps, beside Settings:

- **Skills.** Every skill Frog can use, what each adds to a turn, how often it has been used
  in the last 30 days, and a switch. Open one to read exactly what Frog reads. (#467, #475)
- **Connections** moved here from Settings: each connection's tools as a grid you can
  switch one by one, with tools withheld when their descriptions read like instructions.
  (#473)
- **Rules.** One card per rules file, in this project and everywhere, with Open in editor
  and Show in Finder. (#475, #477)
- **Memory.** The notes Frog keeps, as a list and, with two or more, a map that places notes
  near others that share words. Pairs that seem to disagree are flagged for you to settle.
  (#482, #483)
- On the web these pages draw only what a cloud session can really use, and are mostly empty
  for now. (#469, #471)

## Memory that costs less

- **A small set of notes rides along whole, as before.** Past that, standing guidance
  (corrections, and notes that say always or never) stays in every turn, and the rest are
  brought back only when your message shares words with them. On 50 notes that is about 230
  tokens a call instead of about 1,000. Matching is by wording, not meaning: a note that
  shares no words with your question is not brought back. (#481)

## Transcript

- **Sending lands on the reply.** From anywhere in a conversation, on both apps. A long
  answer holds at its first line so you can read from the top. (#479)
- **A saved conversation opens at rest,** its end first, instead of drawing in piece by
  piece. (#486)
- **Two lines down the column.** Every row's edges line up, measured on both apps. (#470)
- **No separator dots.** Parts of a stats or footer line are set apart by space. (#460, #479)

## Fixes

- Tests, debug builds and builds no longer ask for the login keychain. Frog reads it only
  when a feature needs the secret. (#465)
- The sidebar's sign-in button tells VoiceOver what it offers. (#474)

## Built, not switched on

- **Frog noting things on its own** and **changing settings when you ask in a conversation**
  are in the engine but off in every app, until their approval rules have been tightened and
  reviewed again. (#466, #480, #487 pending)

## Known

- Customize is about to move into Settings as five more sections; the separate mode will go.
- Automations is an empty page: there is no scheduler yet.
- The explainer cards' "How … works" links point at documentation that isn't written yet.
- The Mac's Customize pages have had little hands-on use against real projects.
