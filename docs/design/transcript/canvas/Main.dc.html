Motion

Cross-fade, no travelThe old line fades out over 180 ms as the new one fades in over 240 ms, overlapping. Nothing slides, so the row never changes size or nudges the composer.
A calm cadenceAt most one change every 1.2 s. A paragraph that lands sooner waits its turn; if three land together, only the newest is shown.
First lineFor the first 600 ms the row is just the dot and the label. If the think ends before then, no thought ever shows.
SettlingWhen the answer starts, the thought fades out, the label becomes "Thought for 31s", the dot goes, and the stats move to the turn's footer. 220 ms, one step.
Reduced motionText swaps with no fade and the dot holds its first frame. Everything else is identical.
CostOpacity only: no blur, no layout. Six sessions thinking in the sidebar animate six opacity changes every second or so.
