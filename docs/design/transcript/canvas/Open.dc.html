It never opens itself.Not when thinking runs long, not when it stalls. What you opened stays open for that turn and is remembered when you scroll back. Opening doesn't move the page.The outline grows downward. If you're following the bottom, the view keeps following; if you've scrolled up, nothing shifts under you. Keys.Space or Return opens the focused row, arrows move through the outline, Esc closes one level. ⌥-click opens every paragraph at once. Long thinks stay short.The outline shows at most twelve lines, then "24 more". The full text is one more click and scrolls inside its own box.
