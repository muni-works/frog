7. The turn line, and the waterfall behind it

One small graphic at the right end of the stats bar, always there: the turn drawn left to right in time. A strip coloured by what Frog was doing, with the pace of the model drawn above it. It grows while the turn runs and rests in the footer afterwards. Clicking it opens the waterfall in a panel over the transcript, so the transcript itself stays text and one quiet line.

Live. The line grows as the turn runs, about four times real speed here. It is drawn in the stats' own grey, so it reads as part of the numbers; hover it here and its colours come up. The curve is tokens per second; it sits on the floor while a tool runs.

The transcript stays words.In the transcript the turn is only ever the 20-point line (or a 6-point strip in a fold or a sidebar row). The waterfall never opens inline; it lives in this panel. Rows are the transcript's own steps.Selecting a row shows that step's detail on the right: output for a command, the change for an edit, the rail for a think. "Show in transcript" closes the panel and scrolls to it. Long waits are cut, not scaled.A wait over 30 s is drawn as a short broken segment with its real length written on it, so one coffee break doesn't flatten everything else. Only real data.Bars come from each step's start and end in the session log; the curve from tokens actually received. Nothing is estimated, and a step still running has an open right edge.
