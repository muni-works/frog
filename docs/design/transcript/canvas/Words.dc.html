The filter, in order. 1. Wait for a paragraph to finish. Never show a partial one. 2. Take its first sentence. Cut at the first full stop, or at a colon that ends the line. 3. Drop lead-ins: "Let me", "I should", "Actually,", "Hmm,", "OK so". 4. Skip it if what's left is a list item, code, mostly numbers or symbols, or under three words. 5. Strip markdown and trailing punctuation. One line, ellipsis at the edge.
