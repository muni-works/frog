Rules

  • One component per part. Workspace, Composer and Card are shared; boards import them, never redraw them.
  • Asks are cards, not modals. Approvals, questions, sign-up and out-of-credits sit in the column.
  • One prominent action per view. Everything else is bordered or plain. No glows.
  • Say where it runs. Blue for your device, green for Frog Cloud, every time.
  • Answerable anywhere. Any approval can be answered from the sidebar, iPhone, Watch or the menu bar; the first answer wins.

Decisions

  • The web is cloud-only: each session gets its own Frog Cloud sandbox.
  • Cloud sessions belong to the account and appear on every device; local sessions stay on their Mac.
  • frog.toys opens straight into the app; marketing lives at /mac.
  • iPhone is a full app from the App Store, not a remote.
  • Dark is the default look. Sentence case, no all-caps.
  • Removed: race, the house, the $0.50 trial. Open: onboarding without sign-up.
