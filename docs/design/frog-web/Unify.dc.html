The web takes from the app

  • Liquid Glass for chrome. f-glass: blur 24 + saturate 1.6 over the canvas, solid fallback under Reduce Transparency.
  • Floating sidebar and capsules, not a full-width header bar.
  • The model pill and its place colors: blue on your hardware, green in the cloud.
  • The same transcript parts: tool rows in mono, diff and permission cards, FrogFace moods.
  • Springs: bouncy 0.42s as cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1); fades 120–220ms.

The app takes from the web

  • One centered column at 720, with the composer under it, on every window size.
  • A roomy empty state: the mark, one question, the composer, three ideas. Nothing else.
  • One prominent action per view. Everything else is bordered or plain.
  • Bigger type where it's quiet: 30pt display for empty states and sheet titles; 13pt stays for work.
  • Asks are cards, not modals: sign-in, cloud moments and nudges sit in the column.
