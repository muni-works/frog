What changed from the frog version
  • No sidebar, no "Guesses". The count lives in the diff header.
  • No percentages. Calibrated probability becomes calibrated language: "wasn't sure", "chose between", "couldn't find".
  • Claude is the subject of every sentence, and every flag ends in an offer to check.
  • Steering is one button: "Use this". The branch mechanics are a footnote.
Where it lives
  • Diff: dotted words and a hover card.
  • Permission prompt: one extra line and one extra button, only when a risky argument was uncertain.
  • Next-step box: the count and an offer to resolve it.
  • Settings: one toggle, on by default.
What it hides on purpose
  • Routing fingerprints, state norms, calibration curves. Research tools, not product.
  • Which flags are "forks" vs "unverified". The copy says it; the UI doesn't need a taxonomy.
  • The model's internals. The product promise is honesty, not interpretability.
