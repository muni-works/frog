Privacy

Frog has no telemetry. Here's everything that leaves your machine, and when.

Never collected

  • Your prompts, the model's responses, or anything you type
  • Your code, file names, paths or contents
  • Repository names, branch names or git remotes
  • IP addresses (not stored, not logged by us, not used for counting)
  • Device names, hardware ids, or any id that tells one install from another
  • Cookies or fingerprints on frog.toys

Your model provider

Your prompts and code go to the model you pick, and nowhere else, to get an answer. A local model keeps them on your machine. A cloud model sends them to that provider (Frog Cloud, OpenRouter, OpenAI, …) under its own terms.

What we count

Only from requests that already reach our servers, kept as anonymous totals:

  • Installs: each fetch of the installer or the Mac app download. Path, platform, country and time. Kept three months.
  • Active Macs: each daily update check. App version, country and time. A count of checks, not of people.
  • Website visits: Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies, storage or fingerprinting. Page views, referrer, country, browser and load time, as totals.

The full detail, field by field, is in docs/privacy.md.

Frog Cloud

If you sign up, we keep your account (email, name, sign-in method), your purchases (through Stripe; we never see card details) and per-request usage (model, token counts, cost, timing) so we can bill you. Never the text of requests made with an API key. Sessions you run in Frog Cloud keep their transcript and working files until you delete them, or for 30 days without activity. We don't train models on your code or sell your data. The full account policy is at api.frog.toys/privacy.

Our team gets anonymous notices: a new sign-up's country, credit bought, a plan started or cancelled, and daily totals. No names, emails or ids in any of them.
