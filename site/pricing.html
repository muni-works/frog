Pricing

Local models are free. Frog Cloud is pay per token, in dollars per million tokens.

On your own machines: $0

Models that run on your Mac or on your own nodes (Apple's on-device model, MLX, ollama, LM Studio, tang) cost nothing. No account, no key, no limits beyond your hardware. Using your own provider key (OpenRouter, OpenAI and others) is billed by that provider, not by us.
